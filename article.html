Rwanda: Exploring the Land of a Thousand Hills

From rolling green hills to peaceful lakeside towns, discover a country filled with natural beauty, rich culture, and warm hospitality.

Amara N.

October 10, 2026 · 6 min read

Some places invite you to slow down, look around, and appreciate the journey. Rwanda is one of those places, where green hills stretch into the distance and every road seems to reveal another beautiful view.

A landscape full of surprises

Rwanda is often called the Land of a Thousand Hills. Its rolling landscapes, terraced farms, and winding roads create scenery that changes with every turn. For travelers who enjoy nature, the journey between destinations can be just as memorable as the places themselves.

The country's landscapes range from the volcanic mountains of the northwest to the shores of Lake Kivu in the west. Each region offers its own atmosphere, making it worth taking time to explore beyond the capital city.

Discover life by Lake Kivu

Lake Kivu offers a peaceful escape with lakeside views, small towns, and opportunities to enjoy the water. Visitors can explore the surrounding area, meet local communities, and take in the scenery at a slower pace.

A thoughtful itinerary leaves room for unplanned moments: an afternoon walk, a conversation with a local guide, or a quiet evening watching the sky change color over the lake.

Travel with curiosity and respect

The best travel experiences often begin with an open mind. Learn about local customs, support community businesses, respect natural environments, and take time to understand the places you visit.

Whether you are exploring a busy city or a quiet countryside road, approach each experience with curiosity. You may discover that the most memorable part of a trip is not a landmark, but a moment you did not plan.

A thought for your next journey

Travel slowly, stay curious, and leave every place with a deeper appreciation for the world.
